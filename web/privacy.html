Chipbook

Privacy policy for the Chipbook web and Android app.

Last updated 18 November 2026.

Chipbook keeps a shared record of money raised with friends. It never holds, moves, or processes money, and it has no payments, wallets, or custody features. This page explains what it stores and why.

Who runs it

Chipbook is a personal open-source project. Questions, corrections, and deletion requests go to aaqililyas.101@protonmail.com.

What is stored

  • Account details. Your email address, the sign-in provider you used, and a username you choose. The username is visible to other members of funds you join instead of your email address.
  • Fund records. Fund names, the currency and cadence you pick, optional targets, membership and roles, and invite codes you create.
  • Ledger entries. Amounts, the contributor name typed in, optional reasons, the kind of entry, and timestamps. Approved entries are append-only: they cannot be edited or deleted, and corrections are recorded as new reversal entries.
  • Device storage. Your sign-in session and appearance preference are kept in your browser's local storage or the app's local preferences. No advertising or third-party tracking identifiers are stored.

Why it is stored

Only to run the app: to sign you in, to show a fund's ledger to its members, and to keep the record consistent between them. There is no marketing, profiling, or sale of data.

Who else can see it

Ledger entries and member handles are visible to the other members of the same fund, including invitations you accept. Database access rules restrict each account to the funds it belongs to. If you sign in with Google, Google sees that you authenticated; it does not receive your ledger data.

Services used

  • Supabase hosts the database and authentication. Your account and ledger records are stored there.
  • Vercel serves the web build and records standard request logs.
  • Google provides optional sign-in. You can use an emailed link instead and never involve Google.
  • The email provider used for sign-in links and invites necessarily sees the recipient address and the message.

Cookies and tracking

Chipbook sets no advertising cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. The only client-side storage is what the app needs to keep you signed in and to remember your appearance choice.

Retention and deletion

Records stay while the fund exists. Organizers can archive a fund, which makes it read-only, and can delete a fund that has no entries. To have your account and its data removed, email the address above; archived funds that others rely on may be retained in read-only form.

Children

Chipbook is not directed at children under 13, and accounts should not be created for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Terms of use

Chipbook is provided as-is, without warranty. It is a record-keeping tool, not a financial service, and it is not a substitute for your own records or for a bank statement. You are responsible for the accuracy of what you and your fund's members enter, for keeping invite codes to people you trust, and for using the app lawfully. Do not use it to misrepresent money that changed hands.
