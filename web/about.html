Chipbook

A shared ledger for group fundraising.

Chipbook keeps the record of money raised with friends: who contributed what, what has been approved, and what has been paid out. It never holds, moves, or processes money.

Open Chipbook Privacy policy

It is a record, not a wallet

There are no payments, wallets, balances, or custody features. You and your friends still move money between yourselves; Chipbook just keeps an honest shared note of what happened.

What it does

Track contributions

Each fund has one currency and its own ledger of contributions, pending entries, and payouts.

Approve together

Members can add entries; organizers approve them. Approved entries are append-only, so the record cannot be quietly rewritten.

Correct honestly

Mistakes are fixed with reversal entries that keep a reason and the original amount, instead of editing history.

Share with a role

Invite people as organizers, members, or observers. Everyone sees the same ledger; only organizers can approve or pay out.

How to start

  • Sign in with Google or with a one-time link sent to your email.
  • Create a fund, pick its currency, and choose a weekly, monthly, or one-off cadence.
  • Invite the people collecting with you as organizers, members, or observers.
  • Record contributions as they come in and approve them together.
