A shared ledger for group fundraising.

Chipbook keeps the record of money raised with friends: who gave what, what was approved, and what was paid out. It never holds, moves, or processes money.

Open Chipbook Privacy policy
⛨

It is a record, not a wallet. There are no payments, wallets, balances, or custody features. You and your friends still move money between yourselves; Chipbook just keeps an honest shared note of what happened.

What it does

Everything a group needs to agree on the numbers, and nothing that touches the money itself.

✓

Track contributions

Each fund has one currency and its own ledger of contributions, pending entries, and payouts.

⇄

Approve together

Members add entries; organizers approve them. Approved entries are append-only, so the record cannot be quietly rewritten.

↺

Correct honestly

Mistakes are fixed with reversal entries that keep a reason and the original amount, instead of editing history.

◎

Share with a role

Invite people as organizers, members, or observers. Everyone sees the same ledger; only organizers can approve or pay out.

How to start

No setup, no spreadsheets, no screenshots in the group chat.

Sign in

Use Google or a one-time code sent to your email.

Create a fund

Pick its currency and a weekly, monthly, yearly or one-off cadence, with an optional target.

Invite your people

Organizers, members and observers all see the same ledger from the first entry.

Record and approve

Contributions arrive, organizers approve, and the figures update in the fund's currency.
