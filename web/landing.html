Built for the awkward middle

Someone is holding the money. Everyone else wants to know the numbers are right. Chipbook is the shared answer.

✓

Approvals, not guesswork

Members add entries, organizers approve them. Approved entries are append-only, so the record cannot be quietly rewritten.

↺

Corrections with a paper trail

A mistake becomes a reversal entry with a reason and the original amount attached, so history stays visible instead of vanishing.

⇢

Payouts and targets

Record money leaving the fund against the balance, and track a weekly, monthly, yearly or one-off collection against its target.

⛨

Private by default

Funds are visible only to their members, and people appear by the username they choose rather than their email address.

Three steps to a trustworthy ledger

No setup, no spreadsheets, no screenshots in the group chat.

Create a fund

Pick the currency, the cadence, and an optional target. You become its first organizer.

Invite your people

Invite codes carry a role: organizer, member, or observer. Everyone sees the same ledger from day one.

Record and approve

Contributions arrive, organizers approve, and the raised, paid-out and balance figures update in the fund's currency.

Start your first fund in a minute.

Sign in with Google or a one-time email link. Create a fund, invite the group, and stop keeping the numbers in your head.
